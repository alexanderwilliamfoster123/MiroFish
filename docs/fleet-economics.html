Four ways to run it

24-month net revenue under your market settings. The liquidity a fleet adds is worth far more than the pots it can farm.

Model notes. Pot = two equal stakes; winner receives the pot net of commission. On agent battles the house stakes one side, so its expected take per battle is S·(2p−1+2f(1−p)) — at a 10% commission the fleet must win 44.4% just to match staying out. Churn each month is base churn plus sensitivity × (win rate − 50%) × the share of a trader's battles played against machines; referral acquisition scales with community size and squared retention, so farming players also dries up the pipeline that replaces them. Expected values only: no variance, no seasonality — a planning tool, not a forecast. Fair-play boundary: this models a disclosed fleet with published strategy cards; undisclosed house players aren't a scenario here because they aren't a business.
