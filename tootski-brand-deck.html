02 — ESSENCE

everyday objects,
issued like equipment.

Tootski treats consumer products as standard‑issue gear. Every item looks vacuum‑sealed, serialised, and engineered for a purpose. The brand earns desire through restraint: industrial materials, surgical typography, and the quiet confidence of something built to spec — not to trend.

06 — ART DIRECTION

shot like lab equipment.

001

materials

Vacuum‑sealed mylar, brushed aluminium, stainless, frosted plastics. Texture and crinkle are the hero — let the foil catch light.

002

lighting

Soft studio grey or clean white sweep. Neutral, even, clinical. Hard enough to reveal surface; never moody for its own sake.

003

detailing

Barcodes, QR codes, serial numbers, metric specs, ™ marks. Bureaucratic ornament — the product is documented, not decorated.

07 — WORDMARK
tootski™

Mark = a sealed aperture: a ring with a solid core, echoing a vacuum valve / lens / port. Always paired with the lowercase wordmark and a functional descriptor line beneath.

tootski™ — standard issue · no.001
08 — APPLICATIONS

where it lives.

PKG

packaging

Foil sachets, sealed pouches, machined tins, Pelican‑style cases. Single bold word + spec label + serial.

WEB

digital

White or graphite canvas, oversized lowercase type, monospaced captions, slow product rotations.

OBJ

collateral

Hang‑tags, instruction cards, embossed ™ stickers, numbered editions, anti‑static bags.

09 — PRINCIPLES

keep it issued, not styled.

DO

  • Lead with material — foil, metal, frost.
  • One blunt lowercase word per object.
  • Document everything: serials, specs, codes.
  • Stay monochrome and neutral‑lit.

DON'T

  • Add brand colour or gradients for flair.
  • Use soft, rounded, friendly type.
  • Over‑style lighting or add props.
  • Decorate — let function be the ornament.
