THE INSIGHT

Nobody posts the nicotine. They post the object it comes in.

So we don't sell a pouch. We sell a tin worth photographing — a small, weighty, beautiful thing you want to take out, set down, and be seen with.

“The packaging isn't the wrapper.
The packaging is the marketing.”

THE ONE RULE

Would someone post a photo of the tin — with nothing else in frame?

If the answer is no, it isn't finished. Every decision — material, weight, finish, type, the click of the lid — is judged against that single test.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Everyone else made a medical device. We're making a desirable object.

PRINCIPLES

Would you post it?

DO

  • Make the tin the hero of every shot.
  • Lead with metal, foil, and weight.
  • Document it: serials, mg, batch, ™.
  • Stay monochrome and quiet.

DON'T

  • Look medical, clinical-cheap, or pharma.
  • Add colour, gradients, or cartoon flavour art.
  • Hide the product behind lifestyle clutter.
  • Ship anything you wouldn't photograph.
RESPONSIBILITY

Adults only. By design.

CONTAINS NICOTINE · 21+ · NOT FOR SALE TO MINORS
Nicotine is an addictive chemical. tootski is an adult product, marketed only to existing adult nicotine users. The technical, restrained identity does double duty — it reads as premium and as responsible, never playful or youth-facing. Strength, batch and warnings sit on every pack as part of the aesthetic, not buried.
