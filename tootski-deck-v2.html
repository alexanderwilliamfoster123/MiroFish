SECTION 01the idea.

Standard issue.

Everyday objects, engineered and sealed like field equipment.

POSITIONING

tootski treats consumer products as standard‑issue gear — documented, serialised, and built to spec rather than to trend.

Desire is earned through restraint. Industrial materials, surgical typography, and the quiet confidence of something made to last.

“We don't decorate the product.
The product is the decoration.”

THE SYSTEM IN THREE WORDS

Sealed. Technical. Monochrome.

01 — SEALED

Protected

Vacuum packs, foil, machined housings. Everything looks preserved and purposeful.

02 — TECHNICAL

Documented

Barcodes, serials, specs and ™ marks. Function reads as ornament.

03 — MONOCHROME

Disciplined

Silver, steel, graphite, black. No colour — only material and light.

SECTION 05principles.
PRINCIPLES

Keep it issued, not styled.

DO

  • Lead with material — foil, metal, frost.
  • One blunt lowercase word per object.
  • Document everything: serials, specs, codes.
  • Stay monochrome and neutral‑lit.

DON'T

  • Add brand colour or gradients for flair.
  • Use soft, rounded, friendly type.
  • Over‑style lighting or add props.
  • Decorate — let function be the ornament.
